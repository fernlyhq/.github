# Fernly

**Ideas grow here.**

Fernly is a New Zealand technology venture studio. We develop our own products and ventures, alongside hands-on client work across software, hardware and practical technology solutions.

We start with real problems: understanding how people / companies work, where things get difficult and what would make a useful difference. Sometimes that leads to a client project. Sometimes it becomes a product or a venture of its own.

**Useful technology. Real problems first.**
